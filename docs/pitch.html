The problem

Fresh food dies on a schedule — and the decision window is 30 minutes

  • Every store, every morning: how much to order, what to mark down, what to promote — from memory and spreadsheets.
  • Two leaks: shrink (thrown-away fresh stock) and lost sales (empty shelves on the day demand peaks).
  • The EU wastes 153M tonnes of food a year; the UN target is to halve retail waste by 2030.
  • Enterprise AI category management exists — for big chains, at big-chain prices. Mid-size chains have nothing.
Who we serve
The category manager of a mid-size grocery chain. Not a data scientist. Decides before 7am.
Sources: Feedback EU; UN SDG 12.3; industry shrink ranges 3–6% of fresh sales2 / 8
The product

A brief, not a dashboard

  • Orders for tomorrow — newsvendor targets from quantile forecasts, with the reasons attached.
  • Markdowns before expiry — costed, with recovered value per line.
  • Promotions with measured lift from the store's own history, plus ready-to-print creative.
  • Watchlist where the model is least certain — uncertainty made visible.
  • One click to approve, edit, or flag. Every decision signed into a tamper-evident ledger.
Design principle
If it can't be checked, it isn't written.
Facts come from the forecaster; words come from the model; numbers are verified before display.

Trust is the product

Four checks judges (and retailers) can run

  • Number grounding. Every figure in the copy is verified against the facts payload; failures regenerate once, then fall back to a labelled template. 17/17 figures checked in the demo brief.
  • Out-of-sample forecasts. WAPE 26.7% · skill +27.1% · band coverage 78.8% · bias −1.5% on 54,872 held-out item-days.
  • Tamper-evident ledger. Decisions are SHA-256 chained; the API re-verifies on every read and tests prove tampering is detected.
  • Honest economics. Revenue is not margin: markdowns trade a little revenue for lower waste — the replay shows the trade-off, not just the win.

Why now · why this grows

From one store to a category

  • Wedge: fresh-section decisions for a single store — the highest-shrink, highest-uncertainty category.
  • Expansion: POS/ERP connectors → multi-store roll-up → supplier orders → delivery-note automation (Evolus working prototype in the repo).
  • Timing: EU food-waste regulation, margin pressure, and open-model inference economics crossing the per-store threshold.
  • Model: per-store/month SaaS; inference cost visible and tiny; self-hosting keeps retailer data home.
